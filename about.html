The Kallol Philosophy

Elevating The Everyday Through Timeless High Jewelry

Founded on the premise that fine jewelry should not be sequestered in vaults, Kallol crafts heirloom jewels meant to be lived in, loved daily, and passed across generations.

Our Heritage

Master Goldsmithing Meets Contemporary Grace

Every Kallol piece begins its journey as a hand-drawn gouache sketch in our design studio before being meticulously sculpted by fourth-generation goldsmiths. We blend traditional hand-carved wax casting with laser-precise micro-prong settings to illuminate each gemstone’s natural radiance.

Our artisans dedicate up to 140 hours to a single cocktail ring—perfecting every contour of the comfort-fit shank, balancing weight, and hand-selecting diamonds that match in color, clarity, and scintillation.

Our Commitments

The Kallol Standards of Excellence

💎

100% Certified Conflict-Free

We adhere strictly to the Kimberley Process. Every diamond and precious colored stone above 0.3 carats is accompanied by an individual GIA, IGI, or EGL laboratory certificate.

✨

Recycled 18K Solid Gold

We craft exclusively in solid 18k gold (rose, yellow, and white) and 950 platinum. Never plated, never filled. Our metals are 100% recycled and certified by the Responsible Jewellery Council.

🛡️

Lifetime Atelier Warranty

We stand behind every jewel forever. Enjoy complimentary lifetime prong tightening, annual steam ultrasonic cleaning, and 30-day complimentary ring resizing.
