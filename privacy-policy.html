Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Kallol Haute Joaillerie, we respect your privacy. When you place an order, create an account, or contact our concierge, we collect necessary personal details including your name, email address, contact telephone number, shipping and billing address, and gemstone certification details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to process and fulfill your jewelry acquisitions, generate GIA/EGL authenticity certificates, dispatch insured courier parcels, communicate delivery milestones, and provide after-sale services such as complimentary resizing.

3. Payment Processing & Security

All online financial transactions are processed securely through our RBI-authorized partner, Razorpay. We never store your full credit/debit card numbers, CVV, or banking passwords on our servers. All sensitive transmission is encrypted via 256-bit SSL protocols.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Information is shared only with verified logistics partners (such as Ferrari Express, Blue Dart, FedEx) solely for the purpose of completing insured delivery to your hands.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

For any queries or data deletion requests, contact our concierge at concierge@kallol-jewels.com or phone +91 98765 43210.
