Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. 30-Day Complimentary Returns

We want you to treasure your jewelry piece forever. If for any reason you are not completely enchanted with your acquisition, you may request a return within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange.

2. Return Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for a return, the jewelry piece must be in its original, unworn condition with all accompanying security tags intact, along with the original GIA/EGL laboratory certification and luxury velvet gift packaging.

3. Order Cancellation Window

You may cancel an order free of charge at any time prior to dispatch (typically within 24 hours of placement). Once dispatched via insured courier, the 30-day return policy applies.

4. Refund Timeline & Processing

Once your returned item is received and inspected by our master gemologists (within 48 hours of receipt), your refund will be processed immediately. The refund will be credited back to your original payment method (Bank Account, Credit/Debit Card, or UPI via Razorpay) within 5 to 7 business days.

5. How to Initiate a Return

Simply contact our concierge team at concierge@kallol-jewels.com or call +91 98765 43210. We will arrange a secure, complimentary arm-to-arm insured courier pick-up from your doorstep.
