Logistics & Transit

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Complimentary 100% Insured Delivery

Every Kallol creation is shipped via armored, fully insured courier partners (such as Blue Dart Apex, FedEx Priority, and Ferrari Logistics). Your shipment is 100% insured from our atelier vault until it is safely signed into your hands.

2. Delivery Timelines

• Standard Insured Courier (Complimentary): Dispatched within 24 hours. Delivery takes 2 to 4 business days across metro cities in India, and 3 to 5 business days for other regional destinations.
• Overnight White Glove Courier (₹35.00 / Priority): Delivered next business day morning with direct security verification.
• International Delivery: 4 to 7 business days worldwide.

3. Tracking Your Parcel

Once dispatched, you will receive an automated dispatch confirmation email and SMS containing your live courier tracking link and digital GIA authenticity registry certificate.

4. Discretion & Gifting Packaging

To protect valuable high jewelry against tampering or theft during transit, outer packages are strictly discreet and unbranded. The signature Kallol emerald and gold velvet keepsake box is nestled safely inside.

5. Shipping Queries

For urgent dispatch requests or address modifications, please reach our logistics desk at concierge@kallol-jewels.com or phone +91 98765 43210.
